9. Quiz Mode Selection
After selecting a movie, show the available quiz modes.

Initial prototype modes:

Classic Trivia
General questions about the movie.

Characters
Questions about characters and relationships.

Quotes
Identify characters or scenes from quotes.

What Happens Next?
Questions based on the sequence of events.

Mixed
A combination of all available question types.
